Trying to reconnect."), Redeem (select, confirm dialog, success screen with huge monospace code, INSUFFICIENT_POINTS shows required/balance, BUSY_TRY_AGAIN banner with Try again, other codes via `errorMessage`, button disabled while submitting and single-flight guard), My vouchers (list, tap opens full-screen large code).
